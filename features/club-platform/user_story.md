# Club platform
[Plan](planning.md) · [Request flow](diagram/uml_diagram/sequence.md) · [States](diagram/state.md)

Members discover commands, mention teammates, create and vote in polls, RSVP, flag a message and review their games. Staff schedule events, manage a roster and review an attributable history. Owners transfer control. Site administrators triage abuse across clubs. Captains register eligible players and follow each board through to the final team score.

All writes use the authenticated user. Membership, timeout, visibility and staff scope are checked again when a form is submitted. Use the locked Hội quán components and Vietnamese strings.
