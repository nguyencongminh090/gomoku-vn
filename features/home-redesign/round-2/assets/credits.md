# Tài sản hình ảnh và phông chữ

Hai ảnh dành cho mockup được tạo bằng công cụ ImageGen tích hợp và lưu dạng WebP.

## `son-vermilion.webp`

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Landscape hero image for a premium Vietnamese Gomoku (Caro) web game. A sharply lit overhead editorial still life. A cropped light maple Caro board with a regular fine 15-by-15 square grid enters diagonally from the lower right in a vivid vermilion lacquer-red set. Beautiful polished jet-black and warm ivory lentil game stones sit precisely on grid intersections. Five black stones make a clear diagonal just off center, with a small group of white stones nearby. Keep the main stones and part of the board edge inside the middle 60 percent for both mobile and desktop crops. Dry red pigment texture, warm maple grain, hard side light, long shadows, subtle analog film grain. Exceptional premium product photography. Image only, no people, no props, no lettering, no logos, no UI.

## `finale-blue.webp`

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Wide landscape cinematic website hero for a competitive Gomoku (Caro) game. View close to the surface of a dark smoked wood Caro board, a few black obsidian and smooth ivory lentil stones resting on the fine square grid. A bright cool blue sidelight passes across the center-right group of stones with small warm amber reflections. Focus and strongest visual detail in the center and right. Make the leftmost 35 percent dark and quiet for an overlay heading. Deep midnight navy, steel blue, honey accents, tactile matte wood, convincing dimensions and perspective, horizontal widescreen crop. A restrained dramatic film still, no hands, no text, no logos, no UI.
